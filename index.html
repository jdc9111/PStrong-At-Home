Group Classes Aren't Right For Everyone

The Challenge

  • Getting to a class isn't always easy — transportation, weather, or a busy schedule get in the way.
  • Joint pain, balance concerns, or a recent surgery can make a group setting feel intimidating.
  • You'd rather have a coach's full attention than share one with a room full of people.

The Solution

  • Your coach comes to you — no travel, no scheduling around class times.
  • Every session is one-on-one, and every program is built entirely around your body and goals.
  • Train in a space where you already feel comfortable and safe.

Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to buy or own any equipment?+

Not at all. We provide all the equipment needed for your sessions — we bring it right to your home.

I'm older or out of shape — can I still start?+

Absolutely. You can start strength training at any age. Every program is built around your current ability and progresses safely from there.

Can I participate if I use a cane or walker?+

Yes! We come to you and work with your abilities, building a program around exactly where you're starting from.

How much does it cost?+

Sessions with Bonnie, our Founder and Head Coach, are $165/hour. Sessions with our Registered Kinesiologist are $110/hour.

What areas do you serve?+

We serve Collingwood and the surrounding areas.

Is the introductory call really free?+

Yes. Your first phone call with us is completely free, with no obligation to sign up for anything.

What happens at my first in-home visit?+

We conduct a detailed assessment of your needs, goals, mobility, and health history, so we can build a training program that's right for you.

Is this safe if I have a condition like osteoporosis or arthritis?+

Yes. Bonnie is Bone Fit™ certified through Osteoporosis Canada, and every program is evidence-based and adapted to your individual health considerations.
